01 / ABOUT

More than a stay.
A support system.

Maanavta Hitaay’s public mission describes shelter, healthcare support, counselling and social inclusion for vulnerable people, alongside an old-age-home offering for elderly people who need shelter, medical care, dignity and emotional support.

“Compassion, dignity, and selfless service”Values stated by the organization
Mission

Establish shelter homes, advocate for vulnerable populations and promote social inclusion with stakeholder support.

Vision

Create a future where people facing homelessness can live with dignity, reach their potential and participate in their community.

Approach

Public materials describe healthcare support, counselling, education, skill development and employment-oriented support for vulnerable people.

The organization's published care flow describes five stages.

Contact

Families call the home to understand services and admission information.

Consultation

Ongoing care

08 / FAQ

Questions families
usually ask.

Where is the home located?

The public directory listing gives Staff Colony, Plot B, near Gajanan Maharaj Temple, Sector No. 27, Pradhikaran, Nigdi, Pimpri-Chinchwad, Maharashtra 411044.

How do I enquire about admission?

Call +91 70668 83322. The published flow describes consultation, assessment and a nurse introduction before ongoing medical care.

What care services are listed?

Assisted living, dementia care, palliative care, medical support, meals & nutrition, family consults and rehabilitation are listed publicly.

Are these actual resident or facility photos?

No. Representative images are clearly labelled to avoid presenting unrelated people or facilities as residents of Maanavta Hitaay Seva Chatra.

Can the owner manage resident records here?

Yes. The Owner records area keeps admission records, medical notes, family contacts, follow-ups and room/bed tracking. Records are saved to a private GitHub repository that only the owner's access token can read or write.
